HOW FOODBRIDGE WORKS

From surplus to shared, in four steps.

A clear workflow makes every donation easy to list, discover, collect and complete.

01
🍽️

List surplus

Food providers add safe surplus with quantity, location and expiry.

02
🔎

Discover

Verified NGOs see available food prioritized by expiry.

03
🤝

Accept & collect

An NGO accepts the donation and arranges collection.

04
❤️

Complete

The collection journey is tracked until completion.

WHY FOODBRIDGE

Built around the moments when food matters most.

Safe surplus food can have a short window of usefulness. FoodBridge makes that window visible and actionable.
